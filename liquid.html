iOS Safari “Liquid Glass” Repro Harness

Simulated controls

Toggle the switches and watch for corruption when the video is playing, when entering/exiting full screen, rotating the device, and while the “glass” fades in/out. The default state turns on the combinations most often implicated in iOS CA/WebKit bugs: backdrop-filter over a video, rounded overflow clipping, and a 3D transform that forces the container into its own compositing layer.

If corruption disappears when “backdrop-filter on controls” is off, focus the investigation on the glass layer; if it only reproduces with rounded corners and overflow clipping, it can indicate a CoreAnimation backdrop sampling bug around masked layers; if it depends on translate3d(0,0,0), it may be a compositing order issue.
